## Getting to the Venue

[Wangjiang Hotel](http://www.wangjianghotel.com/index.html)(No.42 Xiashahepu Street, Jinjiang District, 610066, Chengdu, Sichuan, China)is reachable by taxi or metro from the city’s main transport hubs. Use the routes below for the smoothest arrival experience.

### From Chengdu Shuangliu International Airport

**Taxi**

- Distance: ~22 km
- Duration: ~0.5 hour
- Boarding point: Ride-hailing pickup area at Terminal 2

**Metro**

1. Enter at Shuangliu Airport Station (Exit B).
2. Take the Suburban Line to Zhujiang road Station.
3. Transfer to Line 8 toward Dongda road Station.
4. Walk 700 m to the hotel entrance.
5. Total duration: ~1 hours.

### From Chengdu Tianfu International Airport

**Taxi**

- Distance: ~60 km
- Duration: ~1 hour
- Boarding point: Ride-hailing pickup area at Terminal 2

**Metro**

1. Enter at Tianfu Airport Station (Exit B).
2. Take the Suburban Line to South Railway Station.
3. Transfer to Line 1 toward Nijia bridge Station.
4. Transfer to Line 8 toward Dongda road Station.
5. Walk 700 m to the hotel entrance.
6. Total duration: ~1.5 hours.

### From Chengdu East Railway Station

**Taxi**

- Distance: ~3 km
- Duration: ~10 minutes
- Boarding point: Taxi/ride-hailing pickup area outside the station concourse

**Metro**

1. Enter the metro Chengdu East Station.
2. Take Line 2 directly to Tazi Mountain Park Station .
3. Walk 800 m straight to the venue lobby.
4. Total duration: ~20 minutes.
### From Chengdu West Railway Station

**Taxi**

- Distance: ~23 km
- Duration: ~40 minutes
- Boarding point: West Square ground-level parking/taxi area

**Metro**

1. Enter at Chengdu West Railway Station .
2. Take Line 4 toward Provincial Hospital of Traditional Chinese Medicine Station .
3. Transfer to Line 2 toward Dongda road Station.
4. Walk 700 m straight to the venue lobby.
5. Total duration: ~50 minutes

For late-night arrivals, taxis or ride-hailing services remain available around the clock. Please allow extra time during peak commuter hours.
